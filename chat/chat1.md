# Synx Chat Updates 10/1/2026

## Conversations

- Added separate conversations for Drei Zamora, Lebron James, James Ivan, Ryan, and John Mico.
- Each conversation has its own messages and its own latest-message time in the people list.
- Added a typing indicator for active contacts and an empty state when a conversation is deleted.
- You can send messages, share the currently playing song, and play a song shared in chat.

## Replies and Reactions

- Swipe right on someone else's message to reply.
- Swipe left on your own message to reply to yourself.
- Long-press a message on touch devices, or right-click it on desktop, to show reply, reaction, and unsend actions.
- The action toolbar appears inside the selected message and scales it slightly so the controls stay visible.
- You can unsend your own messages.

## Songs and Artwork

- Added songs by LANY, Brent Faiyaz, Drake, Bruno Mars, and PARTYNEXTDOOR to the conversations.
- Replaced generic chat covers with the matching album artwork and album names.
- Album covers use square artwork and show in shared messages and the player.

## Conversation Options

- The conversation menu includes a delete option with a confirmation step.
- Deleting clears only the selected conversation.
- The people list shows each conversation's latest message time.

## Account and Layout

- Added logout from the footer, profile section, and account menus in the lower-left and top-right.
- Logging out returns to the welcome/login screen without clearing saved music or libraries.
- Chat controls wrap on narrow screens, and the dashboard content uses more width on wide displays.
